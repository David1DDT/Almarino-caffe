# Product: Almarino Caffè

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Coffee enthusiasts, artisan café lovers, local visitors, and customers looking for high-grade specialty roasts, pickup & table ordering, and exploring the warm atmosphere of Almarino Caffè in Alba Iulia.

## Product Purpose

A digital storefront and interactive experience for **Almarino Caffè**, a top-rated 5.0★ artisan coffee shop located at Bulevardul 1 Decembrie 1918 M4, Alba Iulia. It combines local café ordering with an interactive 3D roast visualizer and custom coffee experience.

## Positioning

Combines high-touch artisan café hospitality (founded by owner Alexandru) with an interactive 3D roast customizer that lets customers explore roast profiles, specialty beverages, and order directly.

## Operating Context

- Web browser on mobile (in-café QR table ordering / on-the-go pickup) and desktop (menu exploration & bean customization).
- Rich warm brown, espresso, and golden caramel aesthetic matching the cozy artisan coffee atmosphere.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Interactive 3D Roast Visualizer**: Renders real-time 3D cup model, steam physics, and roast profile variations (Light, Medium, Dark).
- **Custom Blend & Beverage Selector**: Selection of grind types, bag weights, espresso variations, and custom label names.
- **Café Pickup & Menu Integration**: Browse specialty drinks, food menu, and location details (Alba Iulia) with online pickup/table order workflows.
- **5.0★ Verified Reviews**: Showcasing real Google & Tripadvisor reviews praising Alexandru's passion and warm hospitality.
