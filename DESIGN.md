# Visual System: Rich Warm Brown & Golden Amber (Almarino Caffè)

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## Color Palette (Warm Espresso & Caramel Luxury)

- **Ground Primary**:
  - Dark Mode: Deep Espresso Midnight (`#1a0f0a` / `#140a06`)
  - Light Mode: Warm Cream Alabaster (`#fdfbf7`)
- **Primary Brand Accents**:
  - Golden Amber Crema (`#d49b4b` light / `#e5ac5d` dark)
  - Roasted Chestnut / Copper (`#b86b32` light / `#cd7b3c` dark)
- **Surfaces & Containers**:
  - Dark Mode: Rich Chocolate Slate (`#25160d` with `rgba(212, 155, 75, 0.25)` border)
  - Light Mode: Warm Porcelain Glass (`#ffffff/90` with `rgba(184, 107, 50, 0.18)` border)
- **Accents & Badges**:
  - Single Origin / Specialty: Golden Caramel (`#d49b4b`)
  - Organic / Reserve: Warm Copper (`#b85232`)
  - Toast & Crema: Cream Vanilla (`#f7ebd9`)

## Typography

- **Headlines**: Elegant High-Contrast Serif (`font-serif font-black`, paired with `italic font-light`).
- **Data & Micro-Labels**: Crisp Monospace tracking (`font-mono text-[10px] tracking-widest uppercase`).
- **Body & Controls**: High-legibility Sans-Serif (`font-sans`, 4.5:1+ WCAG AA contrast).

## Layout & Spatial System

- **Hero Surface**: Asymmetric 50/50 split pairing Almarino Caffè editorial headlines with interactive 3D WebGL espresso viewport framed in roasted amber glass.
- **Fulfillment Integration**: Distinct order fulfillment bar (In-Café Table Order vs Express Pickup vs Delivery) + Slide-Over Basket Drawer.
- **Location Showcase**: Alba Iulia flagship location details (Bulevardul 1 Decembrie 1918 M4), opening hours (21:30 close), and 5.0★ Google reviews.
